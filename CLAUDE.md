# Skyrim running on Apple Vision Pro

The game runs on the headset. What it takes to play: one person, their input devices, one M2 Apple Vision Pro
and, if wanted, wall power. Nothing else. Never propose streaming, or offloading any of
the game's compute to another machine at play time. Other machines may build, test and measure.
